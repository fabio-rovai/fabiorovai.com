01Biography

Formal rigour, applied to messy problems.

Fabio Rovai is an AI researcher and data scientist based in London, founder of Rovai Heavy Industries and co-founder of The Tesseract Academy. He holds an MSc in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence from the University of the Arts London, where he later taught as an Associate Lecturer for four years.

His research addresses a single question: how do you know an AI system's reasoning is sound? That question runs through all of his published work, from causal verification of agent actions before they execute, to ontology alignment that is provably stable, to event-graph substrates that support counterfactual queries.

Alongside the research, he leads applied engagements for public bodies and industry. He has worked as a researcher for the Alan Turing Institute on large language models in cyber defence, produced a published land valuation study for the Welsh Government, led an Innovate UK BridgeAI programme for the creative industries, and built ontology tooling for the National Digital Twin Programme.

He reviews for the NeurIPS ethics track and maintains a set of open-source tools in Rust and C for ontology reasoning, agent provenance, and document verification.

Refereed papers and acceptances

  1. 2026OM 2026

    Consistency Is Not Coherence: Orientation Search for Certified Alignments Between 4D Defence Upper Ontologies

    Long paper, OM 2026, the 21st International Workshop on Ontology Matching, co-located with ISWC 2026, Bari.

    The first public alignment of the IES, HQDM and BFO upper ontologies. A naive strict-equivalence merge stays consistent while filling with empty classes, so the paper repairs by orientation instead of deletion and certifies the bridge that results.

  2. 2026TDI 2026

    Assurance Laundering: A Cross-Standard Ontology and Machine-Checked Weakest-Link Policy for Verifiable Credentials

    International Conference on Trustworthy Digital Infrastructure 2026, hosted by The Alan Turing Institute, London. Presented in person on 16 September 2026.

    Lean theorems, axiom-clean, across eight credential stacks, giving the conditions under which a compliant-looking credential carries no assurance at all.

  3. 2026WASP 2026

    Recovering Method Parameters from Protocol Prose: Task Definition, Audited Extraction, and the Comparability It Licenses

    Workshop paper, WASP 2026, held with AACL-IJCNLP 2026.

    Defines the task of recovering method parameters from the prose of published protocols, audits the extraction, and sets out which comparisons the recovered parameters support.

  4. 2026SEMANTiCS 2026

    Conformance Over Nothing: Validating a National Metadata Profile Against Its Own Published Artifacts

    Accepted demonstration, Practitioners Track, SEMANTiCS 2026, Ghent, 17 September 2026.

Preprints (not refereed)

  1. 2026Preprint

    WorldKernel: A World Model is the Coupling Kernel of Admissible Possible Worlds

    9 June 2026.

    Argues that observational and interventional data given to a strong enough predictor is not sufficient for counterfactual reasoning. Reframes a world model as a positive semidefinite coupling kernel over admissible possible worlds, where the diagonal is ordinary prediction and the off-diagonal carries the cross-world dependence a standard predictor cannot represent.

  2. 2026Preprint

    CIVeX: Causal Intervention Verification for Language Agents

    9 May 2026.

    A verifier that decides whether a tool-using agent should execute a proposed action, by mapping it to a structural causal query over a committed action-state graph and returning an auditable verdict: execute, reject, experiment, or abstain. Reports zero observed false executions under moderate and adversarial confounding.

  3. 2026Preprint

    Open Ontologies: Tool-Augmented Ontology Engineering with Stable Matching Alignment

    9 May 2026.

    An open-source Rust system pairing LLM-driven ontology construction with formal OWL reasoning and alignment over the Model Context Protocol. Stable one-to-one matching is shown to be the dominant factor in alignment quality, reaching F1 of 0.832 on the OAEI Anatomy track.

  4. 2026Preprint

    Deterministic Event-Graph Substrates as World Models for Counterfactual Reasoning

    15 May 2026.

    Treats a deterministic event graph as the substrate of a world model, so that counterfactual questions become graph queries with defined answers rather than sampled generations.

  5. 2026Preprint

    Saturating Scaling Laws for Equational Discovery

    May 2026.

    592 runs across three toy substrates plus two real-world replications, testing a mean-field model of saturating power-law growth. Finds that growth dynamics are substrate-conditional and do not generalise across domains.
