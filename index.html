About

Built different.

Fabio Rovai Ltd is a London-based multi-venture company founded by Fabio Rovai. We don't fit neatly into one category — and that's by design.

We work across AI consulting, education, open-source technology, and applied research. Our projects range from government AI strategy to building developer tools in Rust to reviewing ethics papers for NeurIPS.

We believe the most interesting work happens at the intersection of disciplines. That's where we operate.
